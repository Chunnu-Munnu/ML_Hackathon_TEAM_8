# PathSnap data quality

The notebook inventories 80 training trips and 10 student-test trips. All 360 required files were present and parsed. Training has 208,417 GPS observations and student-test has 19,160. The analyses below use the training partition. [Trip inventory](trip_inventory.csv)

- **GPS fields.** The training tracks contain no missing or infinite GPS fields and no out-of-range coordinates. There were no exact duplicate GPS rows and six consecutive repeated positions. Preserve every observation and use the loader’s duplicate and jump flags. [GPS quality](gps_quality.csv)
- **Sampling.** Timestamps increased within all 80 training trips. Trips `00000002`, `00000071`, and `00000097` contain fractional timestamps. The median trip has 99.14% one-second steps. There are 1,216 intervals over five seconds, including 153,064 s in trip `00000090` and 52,810 s in trip `00000078`. Keep the original timestamps and use the pipeline’s gap flag. [Sampling by trip](sampling_by_trip.csv) · [Largest gaps](largest_gps_gaps.csv)
- **Geography and speed.** Training observations span 100.419°W to 137.237°E and 34.640°S to 61.267°N. The notebook uses M1’s per-trip UTM projection to measure distances. For positive time intervals it flags 150 projected speeds above 70 m/s, with a maximum of 180.54 m/s. The loader uses approximate metres and flags 148 steps. Preserve the points and use M1’s flags as model features. [Geographic extent](geographic_extent.csv) · [Projected speed outliers](speed_outliers.csv)
- **Road network.** Individual networks range from 299 to 155,003 nodes and from 649 to 307,392 directed arcs. Across the training networks there are 1,775 duplicate directed arcs, 22 self-loops, 78 zero-length arcs between distinct nodes, and zero isolated nodes. The median trip’s canonical non-loop reverse-twin share is 95.1%. Use M1’s canonical IDs, directed geometry, and candidate mask. Weak and strong component counts cover all 80 training networks. [Network topology](network_topology.csv)
- **Reference routes.** The median training reference length is 14.31 km. Ten reference routes contain 13 direct connectivity breaks in total. Length sums arcs in recorded order, and the analysis counts discontinuities without inserting segments. [Route lengths and breaks](reference_route_lengths.csv)
- **Alignment.** The point-weighted training median distance to the aligned reference arc is 5.68 m and the 95th percentile is 142.10 m. Trips `00000033` and `00000054` have medians of 122.41 m and 140.09 m. M1’s 50 m trip-median rule excludes them from fitting. Trip `00000002` has a long tail with a 242.36 m p95 and a 5.77 m median, so the rule retains it. The aligned observations remain in the EDA. [Alignment overview](alignment_overview.csv) · [M1 trip alignment](label_alignment.csv)
- **Candidate coverage.** On all training points, the selected 50 m radius and eight-candidate cap miss 24,841 reference arcs (11.92%) at a mean of 6.14 candidates per point averaged across trips. A 75 m radius and cap of 12 miss 11.18% at a mean of 9.31 candidates, which is a 0.74 percentage point improvement with 52% more candidates. The experiments retain M1’s 50 m and eight-candidate configuration. [M1 sweep](candidate_miss_rate.csv) · [Coverage plot](candidate_sweep.png)
